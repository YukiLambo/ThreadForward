Upcycling workshops

Mend it.
Make it
yours.

A 90-minute session on what fast fashion costs — and a hands-on hour giving one of your own pieces a new life with embroidery or patches.

  1. 1

    Understand the problem

    Where clothes come from, where they end up, and what Hong Kong's 400 tonnes a day really looks like.

  2. 2

    Learn a stitch or two

    Sashiko, running stitch and patch-setting. Materials provided; no experience needed.

  3. 3

    Keep it or pass it on

    Take your redesigned piece home, or add it to the rack with its new story on the passport.

Bring: one clean garment you'd like to refresh — a tee, shirt, denim or tote works well.
